Abstract
Android studio is a relatively new IDE (Integrated Development Enviroment) that allows its users to create android applications of all different types of complexity. It holds an infinite amount of possibilities for not only its users but the world as well! Its ability to allow for anyone, not just those educated in software engineering to put their ideas to an actual running app brings the ability to change the world; since, its available for free and at home, ideas that were once unable to come to fruition because of a lack of knowledge or equipment can now be carried out by anyone. This is epic because you never know what someone of any age, gender, country, class, background might be able to come up and the monumental impact that it could have on the world.
The easy flowing UI(User Interface) allows for anyone to use it to design their application. It comes with hints as well as tutorials on how to effectively use it. The fact that the internet is also filled with a vast amount of videos, websites, and tutorials on how to use it, makes it a must have for anyone trying to develop any type of android app. It also comes with the abilty to use either Java or Kotlin for the programming language used to build the majority of your apps internal components.
The UX(User Experience) on it is phenomenal and allows for an easier understanding of its functions and coding in general. Its technological abilities to create functional and complex apps makes it a must need. Android Studio provides all the tools to create an appof any category, no matter the severity of complexity from one as easy as notes to one as complex as Uber or Facebook. There is almost no idea that Android studio does not have the capability of making into actual reality. Android Studio will and ultimately has changed the world of mobile applications
