EV-o-nah

Ev-o-nah Official Website

The problem that Ev-o-nah plans to tackle is to help interested car buyers know whether buying an EV car is the right choice for them financially and/or personally.

This is an important decision to make because buying a vehicle is buying an asset. As well as the government pushing forward the idea of EV cars. New York city has already mandated that cars must be at least hybrids. Gas is increasing every day and EV might be the only type of car for the future. Also individuals must take note of how much EV would save them over gas depending on what insurance costs them, are there EV Fuel pumps near them, do they need to buy at home equipment, and will having an EV car be time consuming for them or will it be worth the money and positive environmental benefits.

New car buyers, prospected car buyers, or even future car buyers are the ones who will benefit the most from this app.

1. EV RANGE CALCULATOR is also an app that helps individuals in seeing if buying an EV car is right for them.
2. EV Genius PRO is also an app that helps individuals buy an EV car near them.

EV Genius Pro has a lot of deficiencies in its app as it doesn't check if buying an EV car is a wise choice for its user, it doesn't provide a way to check if buying an EV is the right choice for you. I intend to address this by making an interface that takes certain information from the user to formulate if buying an EV car is right for you.
EV RANGE CALCULATOR does not check how much your insurance might be raised and how that would factor into the financial decision of buying an EV. It also doesn't check for any EV related discounts or policies in your state. Both issues will try to be addressed with Ev-o-nah by asking information about what state you intend to make the purchase and live in, then assessing the insurance rates for EV drivers in those states for your insurance companies and others, as well as checking for any discounts before formulating a calculation on the cost or savings of an EV car for you.

Is it time for you to buy a car? Are you concerned about gas prices rising? Do you worry that gas powered vehicles are starting to become outdated? Have you been intrigued by the EV movement? Are you not sure about the financial repercussions an EV car will have on you? Are you curious about the amount of EV charging stations around you? Would you be interested in talking to EV dealers or EV drivers near you? If any of these questions rang a bell with you, then Ev-o-nah is the app you need but didn't even know you needed! We will help you in your EV journey and help you make the best decision for yourself, your wallet, and the world!!!

I am interested in using Java with Android Studio for this app. Kotlin could also be used but I do not have experience with Kotlin and would find it difficult to complete this project using it. I like Android studio because of its easy to use UI and ability to create efficient android apps, as well as it hints and the various tutorials on using it.
